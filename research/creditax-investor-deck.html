Why the tax advice layer failed after the 2023 reform

The reform changed the rules, and the advice layer never caught up. The gap gets paid in penalties and interest.

What the reform changed

NTA and NTAA 2023

Two new acts, the Nigeria Tax Act and the Tax Administration Act, replaced the old framework with 17 PAYE bands, a redrawn withholding schedule, and a new filing procedure.

CGT amendments

Capital gains rules for individuals changed along with everything else, and freelancers and small investors were caught off guard.

Deadlines didn’t soften

Every obligation kept its date. Penalties for guessing wrong arrive by post, with interest.

How people cope today

One accountant, if they can afford one

Trusted, expensive and concentrated in cities. The informal majority has no access at all.

Search engines and AI chat

Confident, fluent and unmoored from jurisdiction. Foreign or stale rules look identical to correct ones until they cost a penalty.

FIRS publishes for practitioners

The authoritative source exists: guides, circulars, the statute itself. It is simply not written for the nurse in Surulere or the POS agent in Kano.

Where Creditax enters

Not “file your taxes.” Our wedge is money that has already been earned and already withheld by a client, sitting unclaimed. Our first service, WHT Credit Recovery, matches the deductions to the missing credit notes, assembles the claim schedule, and a verified professional files it. Creditax keeps 5% of what comes back.

The four types of taxpayers in Nigeria, and the record each one is missing

Each one earns a living differently, and each is missing the same thing: a record a lender, client, or regulator can read.

The Employee
PAYE · TCC

They’re taxed at source, but they’re not sure the payroll figures are right. And when a visa or loan asks for a Tax Clearance Certificate, putting one together is a paper chase across employers and banks.

The Trader
INFORMAL INCOME

Real earnings, zero paperwork. The bank sees the account, not the trade. The result is a thin file: no credit history, no pricing power, no record a lender will read.

The SME Owner
VAT · WHT · CIT

Three obligations, one operator, no retainer. Withholding is deducted by clients and rarely reclaimed; VAT is remitted by feel; CAC filings lapse.

The Professional
SUPPLY SIDE

The expertise is there, but clients find it by word of mouth and street sign. Bulk calculations are manual, verification is manual, and the people who need help most can’t tell a real professional from a card in the market.

What they share

Every one of them is missing the same thing: a verifiable record of their compliance. The employee’s filing streak. The trader’s declared income. The SME’s remittance history. The professional’s standing. Today, none of it exists in a form a lender, client, or regulator can read.

Why the platform sees it

Everyone here is already doing the behaviour on Creditax: asking the question, running the calculation, uploading the document, claiming the credit. The record builds itself out of that work. No one fills out a form to create it.

The credit signal lenders are missing: whether you file, pay, and keep records

Open banking shows money moving; nobody scores the discipline behind it. Creditax does.

Two records, one person, no connection

Bank statements show flow: money in, money out. What they don’t show is whether the person files, pays, or keeps records. Consistency is one of the clearest discipline signals a person can show, and it’s something we produce naturally, as a by-product of the work we already host.

That’s why we score it. A compliance-weighted credit health score sits alongside the bank-data factors, clearly labelled as indicative, for education and self-improvement. It’s not a bureau report, and bureau data only comes in through partnership, as the bureau’s own report.

The legal path: sequenced and funded inside the raise

Four stages, each tied to where the product is: company, demo, production, bank deals.

1Now · Company

Register the operating company with the CAC, engage counsel, open the corporate account, and get the company its own TIN. A platform that does taxes files its own paperwork first.

2Demo · Assessment light

A lightweight DPIA, on mock data only, plus a draft privacy notice in English. The demo keeps moving while the gates run in parallel.

3Production · Full gate

Register with the NDPC as a data controller of major importance, complete the full impact assessment, put a Data Protection Officer on paper, and ship notices in four languages.

4Bank deals · Assurance

A penetration test, data-residency answers, and SLA and DPA templates ready for institutional review.

Two lines that never move

The FIRS line

We don’t scrape, and we don’t unofficially integrate with any government system. Only published law and guidance, cited on every answer. General information, never representation: high-stakes matters route to a verified human.

The CBN line

We’re not a licensed credit bureau, and we’re never presented as one. The score is indicative, for education and self-improvement. Bureau data only comes in through partnership, as the bureau’s report. And we don’t lend from our own balance sheet.

Why investors should like the gate

A competitor that skips this carries regulatory debt it never booked. And because the gate is funded and sequenced inside the ₦10,000,000, the launch date is honest.

How we classify, protect, and delete the data we hold

Four classes, four rules each, and the 72-hour path when something goes wrong.

The classification

Restricted, BVN, NIN, TIN, tokens, secretsNEVER RAW
Confidential, statements, filings, credit factorsENCRYPTED
Internal, tickets, counters, reviewsSTAFF ONLY
Public, blog, docs, pro profilesRATE-LIMITED

The locked rules

  • We never store raw BVNs or NINs. We keep the verdict, the provider reference, and a last-three hint. The number itself lives in request memory only.
  • We never see bank credentials. Read-only tokens, encrypted, revocable from the user’s dashboard.
  • Contact details show only to signed-in users, and never appear on public pages. Anti-scrape, by design.
  • We strip identifiers before processing, so personal data never reaches logs or engines unredacted.
Consent is granular, receipts are logged, and export and delete are self-serve, in all four languages.
