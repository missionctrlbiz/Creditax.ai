CREDITAX.AIEXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Executive Summary

The numbers behind the flywheel

Creditax monetises through four tiers of Naira-only subscriptions, identity verification charged per use, guided Skills with fixed prices and success fees, a developer API, and enterprise contracts. Every metered action carries a documented unit cost; every tier has a documented margin band; every acquisition sits inside a documented cost envelope. This document assembles those facts into a Phase 1 budget of ₦10,000,000 and three-phase revenue scenarios.

What the documented facts establish

  • The free tier is a deliberately funded acquisition channel: typical cost about ₦370 per user per month, hard-capped near ₦1,500 by the 60-message lifetime limit and daily quotas, inside a ₦2,000 envelope.
  • Paid tiers are gross-margin positive from day one: Plus around 55% typical, Professional around 73% typical, Enterprise floored at 60%.
  • One Plus conversion contributes roughly ₦2,750 after serving costs and pays for about seven acquired free users.
  • BVN verification is the highest-margin line item in the product: ₦350 against a ₦90 cost.

What the scenarios show

Two Phase 1 (Month 6) scenarios are modelled on the documented conversion mechanic, differing only in how much of the ₦2.5M go-to-market allocation converts into activated users:

How this scales inside the budget

  • Months 1-2 (build): free tiers and sandbox credentials carry development; provider credits absorb early usage.
  • Months 3-6 (demo and pilot): paid tiers engaged progressively; blended run rate of ₦300,000-500,000 per month.
  • Identity spend is demand-driven: the ₦90 cost is only incurred when a paying flow triggers it, recovered at ₦350 per check.

Provider concentration risk is mitigated by per-environment pinning and a fail-closed demo path. The knowledge-base approach avoids document-parsing infrastructure for the tax corpus entirely.

CREDITAX.AIPHASES 2-3
Section 6

Phases 2 and 3: the widening bands

Phases 2 and 3 are described as ranges, not forecasts. Each new stream enters only when its stated prerequisite is true. Ranges below extend the same per-unit economics to larger bases and new lines.

Phase 2: deepening (Months 7-12)

  • Subscriptions compound on the Phase 1 base: Plus and Professional bases grow with retention; annual plans pull cash forward at the documented 20% discount.
  • Identity pay-per-use scales linearly with the paid base; at 2,500 BVN checks the line alone contributes ₦875,000 per month in gross revenue (₦610,000 after unit costs).
  • Marketplace opens: contact reveal and priority placement monetise the professional supply built in Phase 1; take-rate scoped before any spend.
  • API volume tiers begin converting: sandbox graduates to the 5,000 and 50,000 call tiers for fintech and payroll partners.
  • WHT recovery (Skill 01) begins recognising success fees as filings complete through marketplace professionals.

Phase 3: widening (Months 13-18)

  • Enterprise contracts: white-label reports, portfolio scoring for lenders, SSO and SLAs; floored at 60% margin, contracted volumes.
  • The compliance-credit dataset becomes licensable: aggregated, anonymised scoring factors for partners, the asset no competitor accrues.
  • Embedded rails (if scoped): tax and verification rails for other platforms, as an Enterprise add-on. Not promised until scoped (Appendix B of the working paper).
  • Future bets stay gated: Filing Rooms need marketplace supply; Diaspora Desk needs disclaimers and double-taxation depth; Score-Gated Offers need score credibility and a partner pipeline.

Guardrails (locked in the pricing foundation)

  • Eleven-digit BVN format validated before any provider call; credits charged only on provider success; retries throttled; no silent overages except contracted Enterprise
