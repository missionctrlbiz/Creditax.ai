Section 02 · The Gap

The law changed. The answers didn't.

02 / 20
Creditax.ai

What the law changed

NTA & NTAA 2023

New consolidated acts replaced the old framework: 17 PAYE bands, redrawn withholding schedules, and changed filing procedure.

CGT amendments

Capital gains treatment for individuals changed alongside, catching freelancers and small investors off guard.

Deadlines did not get softer

Every obligation kept its date. The penalty for guessing wrong arrives by letter, with interest.

How people actually cope

One accountant, if they are lucky

Trusted, expensive, concentrated in cities. The informal majority has no access at all.

Search engines and AI chat

Confident, fluent, and unmoored from jurisdiction. Foreign or stale rules look identical to correct ones until they cost a penalty.

FIRS publishes, but for practitioners

The authoritative source exists: guides, circulars, the statute itself. It is simply not written for the nurse in Surulere or the POS agent in Kano.

Where Creditax enters

Not "file your taxes." The wedge is money already earned and already withheld by a client, sitting unclaimed. Skill 01 matches the deductions to the missing credit notes, assembles the claim schedule, and a verified professional files it. Creditax keeps 5% of what comes back.

Section 03 · Who Files

Four taxpayers. Four open files.

03 / 20
Creditax.ai
The Employee
PAYE · TCC

Taxed at source, still unsure the payroll figure is right. When a visa or loan needs a Tax Clearance Certificate, the assembly is a paper chase across employers and banks.

The Trader
INFORMAL INCOME

Real earnings, zero paperwork. The bank sees the account, not the trade. The result is a thin file: no credit history, no pricing power, no record a lender will read.

The SME Owner
VAT · WHT · CIT

Three obligations, one operator, no retainer. Withholding is deducted by clients and rarely reclaimed; VAT is remitted by feel; CAC filings lapse.

The Professional
SUPPLY SIDE

The expertise exists; clients arrive by street sign. Bulk calculations are manual, verification is manual, and the people who need help most cannot tell a real pro from a card in the market.

What they share

Every one of them produces the same missing artifact: a verifiable record of compliance. The employee's filing streak, the trader's declared income, the SME's remittance history, the professional's standing: none exists today in a form a lender, client or regulator can read.

Why the platform sees it

Each persona already does the behaviour on Creditax: asks the question, runs the calculation, uploads the document, claims the credit. The record assembles as a by-product of the work. Nobody fills in a form to build it.

Versioned, not vibes

Section 05 · The Missed Signal

Lenders read statements. Not filings.

05 / 20
Creditax.ai

Two records, one person, zero connection

Open-banking statements show flow: money in, money out. They never show whether the person behind the account files, pays, or keeps records. Yet consistency of filing is among the most legible discipline signals an adult produces, and it is generated as a by-product of behaviour the platform already hosts.

Creditax scores it. A compliance-weighted credit health score sits beside the bank-signal factors, labelled plainly: indicative, for education and self-improvement. Not a bureau report. Bureau data enters only through partnership, presented as the bureau's own.

The dataset argument

Every interaction manufactures a record no competitor holds: verified compliance behaviour, linked to identity rails, across tax types and four languages. Money cannot shortcut a year of live filing data. It has to be earned in operation.

Section 08 · The Network

Verified humans, one tap away.

08 / 20
Creditax.ai

How the verified badge is earned

1 · Registry check

The CAC registration number is validated through Mono's Lookup API. No official public CAC API exists, so the registry hit is corroborated: never trusted alone.

2 · Documentary proof

The CAC certificate itself is uploaded and stored against the profile.

3 · Human review

An administrator approves or rejects from a queue. No profile goes live unreviewed.

4 · Gated contact

Phone, email and WhatsApp render only to signed-in users and are excluded from public pages entirely. Anti-scrape by design.

Live marketplace · browse the supply

Discovery that reads intent

"I need a VAT expert near Lagos Island" returns the professionals whose services match, sorted by distance when location is shared. The referral fires at the exact moment the stakes are highest: audit, objection, appeal.

Section 13 · The Moat

Three locks competitors can't copy.

13 / 20
Creditax.ai
1Cited Nigerian depth

Versioned rules, a golden test set of Nigerian tax questions, a confidence bar that refuses to answer below it. The barrier is not the technology. It is the maintenance discipline of keeping published law current, answer by answer.

Bank statements show flow; nothing scores filing behaviour. Every calculation, estimate and verified document deepens an archive that compounds monthly and cannot be bought after the fact.

CAC-checked professionals, login-gated contacts, and practice tooling that makes supply stay. A directory can be scraped. Trust infrastructure cannot.

Two lines that never move

The FIRS line

No scraping or unofficial integration of any government system. Published law and guidance only, cited per answer. General information, never representation: high-stakes matters route to a verified human.

The CBN line

Not a licensed credit bureau, never presented as one. The score is indicative, for education and self-improvement. Bureau data enters only via partnership, as the bureau's report. No lending from our own balance sheet.

Why investors should like the gate

A competitor that skips it carries unbooked regulatory debt. The launch date is honest because the gate is funded and sequenced inside the N10,000,000: compliance is a scheduled workstream, not a hope.

Section 18 · Data Handling

Four data classes. Four rules.

18 / 20
Creditax.ai

The classification

Restricted, BVN, NIN, TIN, tokens, secretsNEVER RAW
Confidential, statements, filings, credit factorsENCRYPTED
Internal, tickets, counters, reviewsSTAFF ONLY
Public, blog, docs, pro profilesRATE-LIMITED

The locked rules

  • Raw BVN and NIN never persisted: verdict, provider reference and last-three hint only; the number lives in request memory.
  • Bank credentials never seen by us: read-only tokens, encrypted, revocable from the user's dashboard.
  • Contacts render only to signed-in users: excluded from public pages entirely, anti-scrape by design.
  • Identifiers stripped before processing: personal data never reaches logs or engines unredacted.
Consent is granular, receipts are logged, and export or delete is self-serve in four languages.
