# Creditax.ai — Investor Outreach Plan (Phase 1 Seed)

**Purpose:** plan and run the first outreach wave to prospective investors for the ₦10,000,000 seed instrument, using the three finished documents (deck, working paper, financial projections) as the evidence kit.

**Status:** drafts ready. No email has been sent and nothing on this system can send email; every send happens from the founder's own mail client. Contact names and addresses are supplied by the founder (none are invented here).

---

## 1. What we are offering, in one paragraph

Creditax.ai bridges Nigerian tax compliance and creditworthiness: cited answers from published Nigerian law, deterministic PAYE/VAT/withholding calculators, a compliance-weighted credit health score, and a verified tax professional marketplace, in English, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo. The demo track is live across consumer, pro, developer, and admin surfaces (48 routes). We are raising a ₦10,000,000 instrument that funds six months to launch, gated on measured checkpoints: demo stable (M3), production data gate (M4), first paying conversions measured (M6), supply proven (M6+).

## 2. The honesty rules (locked, from slide 19 — never violate in outreach)

- We don't have revenue yet. We haven't run a pilot. We don't have signed enterprise contracts.
- Every forward figure is a labelled scenario band, not a commitment.
- The score is indicative, for education and self-improvement. Not a licensed bureau report.
- All figures in naira, always. No dollar figures anywhere.

## 3. Target segments (build the list; 5–10 names per segment to start)

| Segment | Who fits | Where to find them | First-touch draft |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Nigerian seed / pre-seed funds | Funds publicly active in Nigerian fintech, SME infrastructure, or financial inclusion | Portfolio pages of Nigeria-focused funds; fintech event speaker lists; demo-day rosters | Draft B, then C, D, E |
| B. Fintech operator-angels | Founders and executives from Nigerian payments, payroll, lending, or accounting software | Personal networks, portfolio angel lists, X/Twitter follows (the marketing strategy is already Twitter-first) | Draft A (warm) or B (cold) |
| C. Institutional adjacency | Bank innovation leads and bureau partnership contacts; longer cycle, feeds the Enterprise line from Phase 2–3 | Existing warm client leads from the demo pipeline (mvp-demo-plan); CBN open-banking ecosystem events | Draft B, slower cadence |

The four or five warm client leads from the demo pipeline are a separate track (they get the 8-minute demo, not the raise); do not mix the two pitches in one email.

## 4. Evidence kit (what a prospect may be pointed at)

1. `creditax-investor-deck.pdf` — 20 pages, the primary read.
2. `creditax-working-paper.pdf` — 23 pages, the depth document for diligence-minded readers.
3. `creditax-financial-projections.pdf` — 12 pages, unit economics and scenarios.
4. Live product: `https://creditax.missionctrl.com.ng/en` (pricing, marketplace, developers, status all linked from the deck's closing slide).
5. The 8-minute demo script (mvp-demo-plan) — offered only as the second step, never attached to a first touch.

## 5. Sequence and cadence (3–5 touches, increasing gaps, each adds something new)

| Touch | Day | Angle | Draft |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | The gap: reform-era confusion plus the unscored compliance signal | A or B |
| 2 | 4 | The wedge: WHT credit recovery, revenue on day one (5% success fee) | C |
| 3 | 9 | The math: payback identity and the documented unit economics; link the projections | D |
| 4 | 15 | Breakup: honest close, door left open | E |

Rules: one ask per email, interest-based ("worth a look?"), plain text, at most one link, no attachments on touch 1. Subject lines stay short, lowercase, internal-looking. Each follow-up must stand alone.

## 7. Personalization rule (from the cold-email method)

Every first touch gets one personalization line drawn from something real (a portfolio investment, a public post, an event talk), and it must connect to the problem the email describes. If the line could survive being deleted without breaking the email, it is not doing its job.

## 8. Tracking

Keep one row per prospect in a simple sheet: name, segment, source (warm/cold), touch 1 date, last touch, angle sent, reply summary, next action, status (active / waiting / replied / closed). Do not improvise a different cadence per prospect inside a wave; keep one wave uniform so results are readable.

## 9. What is needed from the founder before wave one

1. Contact list: 5–10 names and emails per segment, plus any warm-intro permission.
2. Sender identity: the name, title, and address the emails go out from (a real human address, plain text).
3. Chosen live-product link for the emails (the homepage or a dedicated route).
4. Approval of the drafts above, edited into full founder voice where wanted.

## 10. Sending capability, stated plainly

Nothing in this workspace can send email, and no installed skill sends from personal accounts. The drafts are paste-ready for the founder's mail client. If sending is ever to be automated, the realistic options are a Lark workspace skill (only if MissionCTRL runs Lark), a programmatic mail service such as Cloudflare Email Workers (needs its own setup and domain verification), or a dedicated outreach tool. None are wired up, and none should be, until the founder approves the list and the copy.
