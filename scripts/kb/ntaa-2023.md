# NTAA 2023 — Tax Administration Procedures

The Nigeria Tax Administration Act (NTAA) 2023 repealed the FIRS Act and
establishes the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) and State/Internal
Revenue Services (SIRS/IRS) as the tax authorities.

## Tax Identification

- A **Tax Identification Number (TIN)** is mandatory for all taxpayers.
- For natural persons the TIN can align with the BVN/NIN via the FIRS portal.
- Businesses must register a TIN separate from or linked to their CAC
  registration.

## Registration

- Voluntary registration is available for small taxpayers.
- Taxpayers above the threshold must register before commencing the
  activity.

## Filing Deadlines

| Tax | Frequency | Deadline |
|-----|-----------|----------|
| PAYE | Monthly | 10th of the following month |
| VAT | Monthly | 21st of the following month |
| WHT | Monthly | 21st of the following month |
| CIT (balance) | Annual | 31st October |
| Self-assessment | Annual | 31st October |

## Objection and Appeal

- A taxpayer may file an objection within **30 days** of assessment.
- Appeal to the Tax Appeals Committee, then to the courts if unresolved.
- Payment on the amount under dispute is required to stop penalties.

## Penalties and Interest

- Failure to file: graduated penalty per return.
- Late payment: default interest per month.
- Fraudulent under-reporting carries additional surcharges.

## Compliance Certificates

- **Tax Clearance Certificates (TCC)** are issued to taxpayers in good
  standing and are required for contracts, procurement, and visa processing.
- A TCC reflects all taxes due up to a specified date.
