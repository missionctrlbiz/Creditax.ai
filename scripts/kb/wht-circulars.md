# Withholding Tax (WHT) — Rates and Credit Notes

## What is WHT

Withholding Tax is an advance payment of income tax deducted at source on
certain payments. The paying party (withholding agent) deducts and remits
it to the FIRS.

## Typical WHT Rates (demo reference)

| Payment type | Typical rate |
|--------------|-------------|
| Services / contract payments (goods) | 5%–10% |
| Professional fees | 10% |
| Dividends (Nigerian resident) | 10% |
| Interest on loans | 15% |
| Rent (residential) | 10% (per payment) |
| Management fees / commissions | 10% |

> Rates vary by category and have changed with amendments — always confirm
> against the current FIRS circular before relying on a figure.

## WHT Credit Notes

- The recipient of WHT-deducted payments is entitled to a **WHT credit note**
  from the paying party.
- This credit note allows the recipient to claim the WHT paid as a
  credit against their final tax liability.

## The Unclaimed-Credit Problem

Many businesses deduct WHT from vendors but the vendors **never claim the
credit notes**, so the money is effectively lost to them. A WHT recovery
process matches deductions to missing credit notes and produces a dated
claim schedule that can be filed.

## Remittance

- WHT must be remitted monthly by the **21st** of the following month.
- Retain remittance receipts to evidence the deduction.
