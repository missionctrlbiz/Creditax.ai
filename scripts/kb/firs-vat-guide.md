# VAT in Nigeria — Rate, Registration, Input/Output

## Standard Rate

- Value Added Tax (VAT) is charged at **7.5%** on most taxable supplies.
- The VAT Act as amended applies the 7.5% standard rate to goods and
  services, replacing the earlier 5% rate for most supplies.

## Registration

- Businesses above the VAT registration threshold must register with the
  FIRS (or State IRS where applicable).
- A valid VAT invoice is required for the recipient to claim input VAT.

## Input VAT

- Deductible when the business holds a **valid tax invoice** and the
  purchase is for a taxable business activity.
- Input VAT must be claimed within the prescribed period.

## Output VAT

- Collected from customers on taxable sales and remitted to the
  authority.
- Net VAT payable = Output VAT − Input VAT.

## Exemptions

- Basic food items, medical products, and education-related supplies are
  generally exempt from VAT.
- Exports of goods are treated as zero-rated.

## Filing

- VAT returns are filed monthly by the **21st** of the following month.
- Reconcile the VAT account monthly to avoid carry-forward errors.
