# Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC)

## What It Is

A Tax Clearance Certificate is a document issued by the tax authority
(FIRS or State IRS) confirming that a taxpayer is in good tax compliance
as of a specified date.

## Why It Matters

- Required for **government contracts and procurement**.
- Needed for **company incorporation/updates**, tenders, and some
  visas/business permits.
- Lenders and financial institutions may request it as part of due
  diligence for **loan approval**.
- Signals **reliability and compliance** to clients and partners.

## TCC Readiness Checklist

To be TCC-ready, a taxpayer should have:

- [ ] A valid TIN registered and current.
- [ ] All self-assessment returns filed for the relevant years.
- [ ] PAYE remittances up to date (for employers).
- [ ] VAT returns filed and reconciled.
- [ ] WHT deducted and remitted with credit notes issued.
- [ ] No outstanding balances or penalties (or a settlement plan).
- [ ] Recent Tax Compliance Report (TCR) available.
